Modern Treaty One

Treaty One today

Treaty one today looks much different than it did back then. Many things have changed, and some of these include currency increase, culture change over time, and expectations of the Canadian government. In 1971, it was promised that the first nations peoples would get $5 anually. In 1971, this was a lot of money but now, this can get next to nothing for a traditional family. This can be troubling because although the Canadian government did promise $5 anually, it is expected that this amount would fluctuate with the rising and droping of currency values, but this was not the case.

While the Canadian government claims that the Treaties were to create an equal reward for both the Indigenous peoples and the government, many situations such as this show that this is not really the case, and the Canadian government has much more advantages in terms of the treaty signing that the Indigenous peoples.
